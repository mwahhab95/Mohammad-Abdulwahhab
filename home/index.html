About Me

Academic identity preserved from the original site

With over 25 years of experience in academia, I have dedicated my career to the field of pharmaceutical sciences. I graduated from the Faculty of Pharmacy, Mansoura University in 2000, where I also earned both my Master's and PhD degrees.

My passion lies in making organic chemistry accessible and engaging for pharmacy students. I’m deeply committed to education and strive to simplify complex concepts through clear explanations and interactive learning.

My experience encompasses extensive proficiency in various molecular modeling and drug design software, alongside a nascent understanding of Python programming.

In addition to research and teaching, I develop custom Windows applications tailored to the needs of academics. These tools are designed to enhance productivity, streamline administrative tasks, and support teaching and research activities—for myself and fellow faculty members.
